i hate this room
she holds memories that the strongest of us would want to forget
her floorboards creak in a familiar way
a way that once rhymed with loves shared
she lets the light in when light is not needed
sometimes you need to feel bad or sour
familiar light from under the door
familiar love’s regrets oncemore
kill me again, will you, it didn’t hurt the first time
kill me again, will you, it didn’t hurt the first time

i hate this room
the memories will kill me if i let them
